# Build phase reports

Phases A through G and immersive passes **I1 through I6** are complete for the local game. Safari playback and physical phone testing remain open. Historical phase reports retain the scope and checks at delivery. [Phase G release report](PHASE_G_REPORT.md)

## Current product decision — no level hints

At the user's request on 4 November 2026, level hint controls, the scene hint lamp, showcase auto-guidance, the automatic candidate-number board and live lamp-rule evaluation were removed. Essential puzzle statements, operation instructions and manual undo/reset remain. The removed `HINT` command is rejected. Legacy hint fields are accepted only for save compatibility, and old hint notes are discarded on load/import. Historical reports below describe what was delivered before this revision.

## Current product decision — audio is required

The user's Phase D correction makes sound part of the intended presentation. **Enter the Road with sound** starts and verifies audio before opening the rooms. Every room has original music and ambience. Failed or hanging starts offer Retry and save export; players retain volume and mute controls after entry. Captions and printed clues remain available. Historical Phase C descriptions of optional sound reflect the earlier decision.
