# Character and location design reference

The user requested sprites inspired by the actual show designs. Use recognizable costumes, silhouettes, materials and room motifs when creating the illustrated scenes. Keep the supplied pixel-art references as the medium and composition guide. The library, login mechanisms and greenhouse puzzle remain this game's adaptations.

The [I3 instrument pass](IMMERSIVE_I3_REPORT.md) carries those established scene identities into the working controls: the stone cellar's chalk circle, Jen's pale marble and glass, Alice's walnut/brass recording hardware, and Agatha's plum-lit séance table. The existing costume sprites supply the characters; compact portrait foregrounds leave their faces visible when objects are closed. The floor-seat, measuring, recording-number and logical-lamp mechanisms are this game's authored interactions. Their source and reuse are recorded in [asset provenance](ASSET_PROVENANCE.md).

## Character production brief

Agatha's base Road sprite should have her tailored navy/teal travel coat, white shirt, visible violet lining, wavy brown hair and a confident, slightly theatrical pose. D23's costume interview and photographs identify these costume features; use them when reviewing the sprite rather than a generic pointed-hat witch. [D23 costume interview](https://d23.com/behind-the-seams-with-agatha-all-along-costumer-daniel-selon/)

The same D23 production photographs show Lilia's mustard-and-blue layers, Jen's pale pink outfit and Sharon's floral dress/flowered straw hat. Preserve those distinctions in their base coven tokens and portraits. Room-specific costumes can be authored as additional variants once their trial art is produced. [D23 production photographs](https://d23.com/behind-the-seams-with-agatha-all-along-costumer-daniel-selon/)

Rio's dark hair, emerald botanical costume details and small red flower follow the user's `death.jpeg` portrait. Agatha's blue/purple color relationships and ribbon gesture follow `agatha.jpg`. Keep those reference links in each asset's provenance. Alice's music/protection identity should remain distinct from Jen's potions and Lilia's divination; Disney identifies those specialties in its production feature. [Disney production feature](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/a-magical-look-at-the-making-of-agatha-all-along-with-kathryn-hahn-and-jac-schaeffer/)

Produce separate scene sprites and dialogue portraits. Review face/hair silhouette, costume cut and characteristic props before adding animation frames. The first Agatha action variants are idle, skeptical, reaching for the dots and pocketing them; Rio's first action is offering the flower.

## Location production brief

| Game location | Design reference and proposed translation |
|---|---|
| Cottage/library entrance | Retain the user's ivy-covered cottage, amber windows and dusk palette. This is the game's fictional entrance; connect it to the Road through knotted roots, purple forest light and practical-looking materials. |
| Coven threshold | Use a stone basement, household clutter and a five-sided floor motif. The show's concept artist describes these features in the basement design. [Pat Presley's basement concept](https://phattro.artstation.com/projects/AZyzK5) |
| Kitchen/studio/cabin | Give each trial a distinct room identity and era, rather than repeating the same potion room. Use the show's production-design portfolio as the visual review reference, then compose the game controls at readable scale. [May Mitchell's production portfolio](https://www.maymitchell.com/film-television/agathaallalong), [cabin/interior design portfolio](https://www.enwdesign.com/projects/agatha-all-along) |
| Tower | Use a circular stone chamber and a tarot table, with vertical architectural rhythm. Adapt the show's castle concept into a compact detail view with the table nick and seat labels required by the puzzle. [Pat Presley's fourth-trial concept](https://phattro.artstation.com/projects/EzEkDe?album_id=1168988) |
| Road and garden | Use twisting roots, layered trees and small pools of supernatural light. The show's practical, earth-connected visual approach informs the material treatment; mint mushrooms and path readability follow the user's garden reference. [Disney practical-effects feature](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/a-magical-look-at-the-making-of-agatha-all-along-with-kathryn-hahn-and-jac-schaeffer/) |
| Rio's greenhouse | A game-specific extension of the botanical portrait: fern shapes, vines, soil, weathered stone and the red flower. Do not describe this room as a literal reconstruction of a show set. |

These are production directions and interpretations, not claims that every proposed game prop or puzzle appears in the show. Each art phase should include an in-game review against this brief and the supplied pictures, at both desktop and phone viewing sizes.

## Phase C interpretation

The kitchen uses blue-green cabinetry, pale work surfaces, warm practical lamps and an uncanny water view as a cozy adaptation of the beach-mansion trial. The studio uses walnut panels, russet fabrics, geometric amber glazing and vintage recording equipment. The art director's portfolio separately identifies the beach mansion and retro house/recording studio sets; their visual separation informs the game's two room palettes. [May Mitchell's production portfolio](https://www.maymitchell.com/film-television/agathaallalong)

Jen's figure keeps the pink Road-inspired outfit; Alice's figure uses red-streaked dark hair, a dark jacket, pendant layers and a vinyl prop. The vinyl pose and matching pixel-art treatment are game adaptations. These first character assets are single poses with procedural motion and caption reactions, ready for further pose/expression production. The [saved prompt set](PHASE_C_ASSET_PROMPTS.json) documents exactly what was requested from built-in ImageGen.

## Phase D interpretation

The cabin translates the show's timber interior into warm paneling, a low séance table, plum fabrics and violet window light. The show's art director separately documents the cabin set and credits its designer, Bob Fechtman. The table, four logical lamps and game planchette are this game's composition. [Enna Chow's cabin portfolio](https://www.enwdesign.com/projects/agatha-all-along)

Lilia's sprite keeps the mustard-and-blue Road costume described in Disney's production photograph, with a layered indigo wrap, pendants and a blank card for the game's divination pose. This is a Road-costume adaptation rather than her trial-specific storybook costume. [D23 costume photograph](https://d23.com/behind-the-seams-with-agatha-all-along-costumer-daniel-selon/)

The tower uses a stone rotunda, Gothic windows, a sword-lined ceiling and chained books as a compact game interpretation of the divination trial. Pat Presley's published fourth-trial concept supports the castle/tower identity; the purple table, appointment envelope, nick and memory scenes are authored for the game. [Pat Presley's fourth-trial concept](https://phattro.artstation.com/projects/EzEkDe?album_id=1168988)

The [Phase D prompt set](PHASE_D_ASSET_PROMPTS.json) records the final built-in ImageGen requests and saved paths. Review uses in-game desktop and portrait-phone screenshots, including both seats of the enlarged card table.

## Phase E interpretation

Rio uses long dark hair, green eye makeup, dark green botanical layers and the red flower from the user's pixel portrait. Disney identifies Aubrey Plaza's Rio as the Green Witch; the generated pose, offering a flower toward the game board, is authored for this game. [Disney production feature](https://thewaltdisneycompany.com/news/a-magical-look-at-the-making-of-agatha-all-along-with-kathryn-hahn-and-jac-schaeffer/)

The greenhouse extends those botanical motifs into iron/glass arches, roots, moss and moonlit plants. It is an original game location. The cottage bedroom/study and after-hours library retain the supplied cottage's timber, ivy, warm lamps and purple twilight. Their props and layout support the game's return reveal rather than reconstructing a show room.

The added threshold cellar follows the practical stone-basement/household-clutter direction documented in the show's art department portfolio, with a root-lined passage connecting it to the Road. [Enna Chow's basement portfolio](https://www.enwdesign.com/projects/agatha-all-along)

The [Phase E prompt set](PHASE_E_ASSET_PROMPTS.json) records all final prompts and saved paths. The cottage uses one unchanged room image and the same prop/embroidery geometry before and after the return; the enlarged rug and receipt share their five interface emblems. Rio and the existing Agatha sprite remain single poses pending further animation production.

## Immersive I4 interpretation

Lilia keeps her established mustard dress, blue patterned shawl and silver curls beside the wood-and-felt two-chair table. Rio keeps the green botanical layers, dark hair/eye makeup and red flower beside the vine bed. Existing sprites are reused; the native chair, card, stone, vine and cap drawings are game instruments with live labels.

The new garden uses the supplied `witch.png` reference for pond, winding path and mint accents, with the established cottage art's twisting roots, plum outlines, violet woodland depth and amber window light. It is an original moonlit game location. Its wide and portrait paintings were generated separately, with a clear foreground for the native cap ring and no second ring baked into the image.
